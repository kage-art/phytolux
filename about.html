About Phytolux Bio Science

Phytolux Bio Science Private Limited is an agriculture-focused company working in the field of bio-fertilizers, agricultural inputs and sustainable crop management solutions.

Our Objective

Science-Based Agricultural Inputs for Better Soil & Crop Practices

Our primary objective is to provide reliable and science-based agricultural products that support better soil and crop management practices. Modern intensive farming faces severe challenges: declining organic carbon, chemical fertilizer saturation, and soil hardening.

By developing high-potency bacterial inoculants, plant-derived bio-stimulants, and balanced micronutrients, Phytolux restores natural nutrient cycling pathways in the rhizosphere, cutting farmer input costs while elevating long-term soil fertility.
