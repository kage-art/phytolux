Quality & Responsible Agriculture

Verified Quality & Compliance

Phytolux Bio Science Private Limited maintains strict adherence to agricultural standards and regulatory frameworks. We only present certifications, registrations, and approvals that are genuinely verified and audited.

1. Product Quality

Every batch undergoes automated hemocytometer optical density cell counts and serial dilution plating. We verify that all liquid formulations maintain a minimum viable population of 1 × 10⁸ CFU/ml at packaging.

  • Zero pathogen or antagonistic fungal contamination
  • pH buffer stability across wide storage ranges (pH 6.5–7.5)
  • Batch incubation trials up to 45°C to test survival under Indian climate conditions

2. Sterile Bio-Fermentation

Manufactured inside sterile, automated, food-grade SS316 closed bioreactors. Clean-In-Place (CIP) and Sterilize-In-Place (SIP) systems prevent any wild bacterial or weed seed infiltration.

  • Automated dissolved oxygen (DO) and nutrient feeding control
  • HEPA filtered air injection for pure aerobic respiration
  • Induction-sealed tamper-proof packaging line

3. Product Documentation

Every commercial consignment is accompanied by verified documentation: Certificates of Analysis (COA), Technical Data Sheets (TDS), and complete batch manufacturing records.

Dealers and institutional buyers can access verified laboratory test reports directly via our technical desk.

4. Verified Regulatory Compliances

We only display registrations, approvals, and quality certifications that are genuine, audited, and currently valid:

  • FCO 1985 (Fertilizer Control Order): Bio-fertilizer specifications strictly adhering to Schedule III standards enacted by the Ministry of Agriculture.
  • ISO 9001:2015: Quality Management System certified for microbiological production and cold-chain distribution.
  • MSME & Corporate Affairs: Registered under the Companies Act with clear corporate transparency.
